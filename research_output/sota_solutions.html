2. Five Mitigation Themes (T2–T6) evidence strength + key numbers

T2. Spatial / Antenna Array Strong

RF Stage · 10 sources · Levels III–VII
Most mature theme. CRPA replaces single antenna with array; complex weights steer pattern nulls onto jammers while keeping gain toward satellites. Three independent 2016 groups converged on MVDR.
Null depth (ideal)
> 40 dB HIL (Fernández-Prades, Broumandan, Cuntz 2016)
Null depth (real)
20–25 dB after calibration drift
J/N reduction

Failure mode

Phase/amplitude calibration binds real performance — mutual coupling, cable mismatch, thermal drift. Blind beamforming injects carrier-phase bias (RTK/PPP-relevant). Pulsed/DME column = Gap.

T3. Pre-Correlation Excision Mod–Strong

ADC Stage · 9 sources · Levels II–VII
Only theme with field-level evidence for chirp and pulsed jammers. Operates before code correlation — imperfections propagate downstream. Three substreams: pulse blanking, FDAF, wavelet.
Blanking + FDAF
C/N0 within 2 dB of interference-free (Gao 2013, Level II flight test, Frankfurt DME)
Blanking for chirp
10–15 dB J/N (Borio 2016, Sim)
RIM/ZMNL acq
Within 2 dB of clean ref at J/N ≤ 30 dB
Signum ZMNL
Reduces pulse to ~3 dB noise elevation (vs 20–30 dB unmitigated)
WPT dual-stage
+15 dB over single-stage (Sim)
Key threshold

Above 60% blanking duty cycle L5/E5a correlation degrades to L1 C/A parity (Kim 2025) — kills L5 advantage exactly in dense DME airspace where most needed.

T4. Baseband (ANF, STFT, RIM) Moderate

Correlator Stage · 10 sources · Levels III–VII
Most theoretically unified theme — Borio & Closas (2019) RIM/ZMNL framework subsumes ANF, blanking, wavelet. Direct ANF-vs-RIM comparison reveals operational asymmetry.
ANF bias
1–5 m common-mode pseudorange delay under strong jamming (Borio & Gioia 2021, USRP2 HIL)
RIM bias
No pseudorange bias (same setup)
ANF breakdown
Linear approx fails at J/N > 40 dB
STFT vs ANF
STFT wins for chirp/FMCW (resolves sweep in TF plane)
Multi-constellation
~50% position-err reduction under RIM (single-group JRC HIL)
Operational implication

For RTK, PPP, timing receivers, dual-freq iono correction: do NOT use ANF as sole mitigation at J/N > 30 dB. Use RIM (signum / Huber ZMNL). Critical for railway signalling, maritime, aviation precision approach.

T5. Tracking + Navigation Filter Moderate

Tracking + Nav Stages · 9 sources · Levels II–VII
Widest architectural range — correlator bandwidth → vector tracking → tightly/deeply coupled GNSS/INS. Cumulative gains when stages integrated; cascade failures when not.
LBCA / DSKF
Phase tracking err ↓ 30–50%; lock 6 dB longer at J/N=30 dB (Cortés 2021/2023, Sim)
Vector tracking
+3–5 dB J/N over scalar (Lashley & Bevly 2010, legacy)
Deep INS coupling
+5–8 dB beyond vector (legacy, 2010)
MEMS bridging limit
~35 s max; drift 1–2 m/s
Cascaded layers
RIM+FDE+RAIM survives any single layer fail (Gioia & Borio 2023)
Vector-tracking risk

Inter-channel coupling propagates corrupted data from partially jammed channel to all others — "contamination" failure mode. Chirp column = Gap despite chirp being top commercial threat.

T6. ML / DL Cross-Cutting Emerging

All stages · 8 sources · Levels V–VII (ALL Sim)
Fastest-growing theme, weakest evidence tier. Zero HIL or field validation. Sim-to-reality gap = field's #1 open problem (Radoš 2024).

Misclass rate

Failure modes invisible to Sim

(a) AGC compression alters feature statistics; (b) multipath time-varying amplitude/phase; (c) front-end IF waveform variability across receivers; (d) ImageNet transfer mismatch (RF spectrograms ≠ natural images); (e) open-set failure — novel SDR jammer gets confidently misclassified.

4. Platform-Differentiated Deployment Stack §7.3 — matched to SWaP + accuracy

Handheld / Consumer

tiny SWaP, single antenna, sub-m goal
  • RIM (signum ZMNL) at baseband highest rejection, no pseudorange bias
  • Multi-constellation passive diversity GPS + Galileo + BeiDou
  • ANF as sole mitigation introduces 1–5 m bias

Vehicular

multi-freq feasible, MEMS available
  • RIM at baseband
  • LBCA or DSKF tracking 30–50% phase err reduction
  • MEMS INS tight coupling bridge brief outages
  • L1/L5 multi-freq + multi-constellation
  • L5 blanking duty-cycle monitor watch > 60% near DME

Avionics (highest stakes)

GBAS, SBAS, precision approach
  • CRPA null-steering at antenna 30–40 dB J/N reduction
  • Hybrid blanking + FDAF Gao 2013 flight-proven for DME
  • RIM at correlator
  • Vector tracking
  • Tightly coupled INS bridging during outage
  • ANF sole baseband mitigation fails precision approach bias budget

Timing (telecom, grid)

absolute pseudorange = clock
  • RIM over ANF ANF common-mode bias corrupts clock
  • Multi-constellation holdover
  • Oscillator holdover at high J/N front-end saturation regime
  • ANF in known jamming regions Borio & Gioia GPS Solutions 2021

5. Cumulative-Gain Principle §7.1 — receiver chain as coupled system

No single technique is sufficient across full civilian threat spectrum (Morales-Ferre 2020). Each stage extends composite operating range. Gains compound when stages integrated.

Stage 1 — Spatial CRPA reduces J/N by 30–40 dB for directional jammers
Stage 2 — Pre-correlation blanking + FDAF handles residual pulsed/chirp energy
Stage 3 — RIM at correlator addresses narrowband residuals, no pseudorange bias
Stage 4 — Vector tracking maintains lock through inter-channel state sharing
Stage 5 — INS aiding bridges brief outages (≤ 35 s MEMS)
⚠ Gap: No corpus study demonstrates full architecture vs high-power wideband at field level. Most consequential experimental gap in 2026 SOTA.
